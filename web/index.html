RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Ground cards fight ground cards. Fleets fight fleets.

Both arenas use the same stat scale. Rarity and combat capability scale attack and health; roles trade damage, durability and initiative. Capability reflects skill, Force powers or other abilities, feats and the depicted phase. Narrative prominence, screen time, popularity and physical size add no power.

FIELD MANUAL / KD–01

Your next discovery.

Encounters arrive on a hidden, variable schedule: by default, a 12–20 minute base window, with automatic cards requiring at least one person chatting in approved public channels within the last five minutes, after the previous drop. Quiet chat means no automatic cards. Eligible contributions shorten the wait by five seconds each, at most one minute total. Each encounter shows its collector limit. The first distinct players to identify it can each collect one copy until its slots fill. After two consecutive successful captures in the server, wait for another member to capture; their second-slot claim counts too. Use the accepted names shown on every card. Chat can bring an encounter earlier for everyone, but does not change anyone’s rarity odds.

Collector limits depend on rarity and edition; each encounter displays its available slots.

Build a team with a purpose.

Strikers deal damage. Guardians hold the line. Support keeps a team alive. Tacticians create advantages. Ground and fleet battles use separate rosters of up to three distinct card types.

The approved Old Republic game cards use the regular pool and keep their Legends provenance visible. Other Legends cards require an explicitly enabled event, a release window and a lifetime supply cap.

Two cards from one allied faction gain +5% attack and health; three gain +10%. Independent and Wildlife are neutral classifications and receive no faction bonus. Choose a role mix rather than relying on rarity alone.

Cards and trades belong to your private Discord collection. This site exposes the public catalog, never player inventory. Commands shown here are the bot’s configured command reference; live availability follows the Discord deployment.

SCARCITY / EXPLAINED

Rarity first. Card second.

Each encounter first draws a rarity, then chooses a card within that tier. Adding more cards to a tier does not increase that tier’s odds.

These are conditional catalog odds using the published release activation and date, assuming available supply. Enabled limited editions enter ordinary encounters during their window. Approved Old Republic game cards are included in these ordinary-pool odds. Other Legends/event-exclusive cards require a separately enabled event and are excluded. The archive cannot inspect private minted copies or reserved slots. The live bot checks both against each lifetime cap; exhausted stock can remove a card, release group or tier, changing actual odds. The final remaining copies may also reduce an encounter’s collector slots.

Every encounter is an independent draw; there is no hidden pity guarantee. Allowing several collectors to claim one encounter does not create extra rarity rolls.

Release starts are inclusive; ends are exclusive. Disabled, future, ended and undated limited editions do not receive a catalog rate. The public archive does not expose player data or private mint counters.

Rarity and combat capability scale attack and health. Roles retain distinct strengths, and speed stays role-based. Narrative prominence, screen time, popularity and physical size add no power. Roles and abilities can change matchup outcomes.
