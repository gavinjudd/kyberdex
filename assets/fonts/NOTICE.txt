BarlowCondensed-ExtraBold.otf is an unchanged copy of the selected source.
Barlow-Regular.otf is the selected WOFF2 decompressed to its original
OpenType CFF/SFNT container. CFF outline bytes, glyph order, cmap, names,
metrics, GSUB, GPOS and GDEF were verified unchanged. The font is not TrueType.
No typeface was redrawn, subsetted, renamed or otherwise modified.
Both original Type Evidence IDs, source hashes/provenance and prepared hashes
are preserved in manifest.json. No Univers or Franklin font is included.
